Privacy Policy

Tracebook · Last updated September 17, 2026

Tracebook is a personal journal. You tell it about your day, by voice or by typing, and it turns what you said into short "threads" sorted into books you can look back on. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it the way we would want to read it.

The short version

  • We do not keep what you say or type. Your voice and your typed words are used once, to make threads, and then they are gone — not the recording, not the transcript, not the text.
  • What we keep is what you see. Your threads and your books, exactly as they appear in the app. If it isn't on a page in Tracebook, we don't have it — apart from the small amount of housekeeping listed below, which never contains your words.
  • You can use Tracebook without an account; then everything stays on your phone. If you sign in (with Apple, Google, or an email link), your books and threads sync to our cloud so they follow you between devices.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

What we do not keep

Your voice. By default your iPhone turns your speech into text using Apple's speech recognition (which may process the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy terms). Some accounts send the recording to our server instead, where an AI service (Google's Gemini API) turns it into threads. Either way the recording is deleted from your phone as soon as it has been used, and we never store it.

Your raw words. The text of what you said or typed is sent to our server and on to Gemini to be turned into threads, and is not stored after that. What remains is the threads — the short facts you see on the day's page — not the sentence they came from.

The one exception, and you can see it. When you design a Custom book, the description you give (and any answers) is kept as part of that book's design, so the book can show you how it was made. It is on the book's How this book works page, and it goes when the book does.

What we keep — all of it visible in the app

Your threads. For each thread: the book and group it belongs to (a category such as Food, or a subject such as Programming or Migraine), its short value, its label, and, if you mentioned them, a time, a duration and a note. Exactly what the day's page shows; you can edit or delete any of it.

A search fingerprint for each thread (accounts only). So that search can find threads by meaning and not only by the exact word, each thread in your account also carries a numerical fingerprint of its text, made by the AI service when the thread is saved. It cannot be turned back into words, it is stored with the thread and deleted with it, and guests' threads never get one.

Your books. The name, colour, icon and template of each book; the subject names a book has used, so they stay consistent; and, for a Custom book, its design (summary, grouping, labels and the description above). All of it is on the book's page.

My List. Suggestions you save in the Inspire tab.

Look back headlines (accounts only). The two or three sentences at the top of a book's look back, written by the AI service from that period's threads and kept so the page opens instantly; rewritten when the threads change, and shown on the page itself.

Account and profile. If you sign in, we receive a unique account identifier and the email address you choose to share (Apple lets you hide your real address). We do not receive your password. A nickname and an avatar you pick are stored with your profile and shown on the Me tab.

Housekeeping we keep that is not on a page

Two kinds of record, neither of which contains anything you said:

Inspire history. Which suggestions you have already been shown (their kind and title), kept for about 45 days so they are not repeated.

Usage records. For each request to the AI service, a technical note: which feature ran, the template, the size of the input, the number of tokens used, how long it took and whether it succeeded — and, for requests made without an account, a one-way hash of the device's network address (not the address itself) so that one device cannot use up everyone's share. Never the words, never the threads. These are kept, without your account identifier, after an account is deleted.

Nothing else. Tracebook does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device advertising identifiers, and does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and never leave it.

How we use it

  • To show you your own books, threads and saved list, and to keep them in sync across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To design a Custom book from your description, and to sort what you say into the right book, group and labels.
  • To write a book's look back for accounts: the period's threads for that book are sent to the AI service to produce the headline; the facts under it are counted on your phone.
  • To find threads when you search: exact matches always, and for accounts also threads that mean the same thing, using the fingerprints above and a fingerprint of what you typed in the search box (not stored).
  • To generate suggestions in the Inspire tab. Your recent threads (from the last two weeks, across your books) are sent to the AI service to produce a few recommendations. This feature is only available when you are signed in.
  • To fetch artwork for those suggestions. We look up covers and photos using only the title of the recommended book, film, album, podcast or a short generic phrase such as "tennis court" — never your threads or your identity.
  • To keep the service healthy: the usage records above help us spot errors and manage costs.
  • To respond when you contact us.

Where your information lives

On your phone. As a guest, everything is stored locally on your device and nowhere else. If you later sign in, the books and threads on the phone are copied to your account once and then removed from local storage.

In our cloud. When you are signed in, your books, threads, saved list and Inspire history are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Access is restricted to your own account.

In backups. The database is copied every night and each copy is kept for 14 days, so something you delete can persist in a backup for up to 14 days before it is gone for good.

Services we rely on

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, on-device speech recognition, and app distribution through the App Store and TestFlight.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, if you choose it.
  • Supabase — database, authentication, backups and the server functions that process your entries.
  • Google Gemini API — turns your speech or text into threads, designs Custom books and produces Inspire suggestions. Requests are sent from our server; Google's use of API data is governed by its Gemini API terms.
  • Open Library, Google Books, Apple's iTunes Search, and Pexels or Unsplash — artwork for Inspire cards, queried with titles only.

Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy policy. We do not share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Your books, threads and saved list are kept until you delete them or delete your account (plus up to 14 days in backups). Voice recordings and raw text are not retained after processing. Inspire history is kept for about 45 days; Inspire suggestions are cached on your device for a day. Usage records are kept without your identifier.

Your choices

  • Use it without an account. Guest mode keeps everything on your phone.
  • Edit or delete threads at any time from the Trace tab. Deleting a book deletes every thread in it.
  • Export your data from your account card on the Me tab, as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a full backup (JSON).
  • Sign out from the Account screen. Your cloud data stays in your account until you delete it.
  • Delete your account. Open your account card on the Me tab, then Advanced → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your account and every book, thread and saved item in it. You can also email us at babyrockproject@gmail.com and we will do it for you.

Children

Tracebook is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Cloud data is protected by row-level access rules so only your signed-in account can read or change it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.

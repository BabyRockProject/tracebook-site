Privacy Policy

Tracebook · Last updated September 12, 2026

Tracebook is a personal journal. You tell it about your day, by voice or by typing, and it turns what you said into short "moments" you can look back on. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it the way we would want to read it.

The short version

  • You can use Tracebook without an account. In that case your moments stay on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Apple, your moments sync to our cloud so they follow you between devices.
  • What you say or type is sent to an AI service (Google Gemini) to be turned into moments. Voice recordings are processed and not kept.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

What we collect

Your moments. When you describe your day, we keep the result: a short activity label, a category (Food, Routine, Exercise, Location, Wellbeing, Other), and, if you mentioned them, a time, a duration and a note. You can edit or delete any moment.

Voice recordings and typed text. What you record or type is sent to our server and on to Google's Gemini API so it can be understood and structured. We do not store the audio or the raw text after this processing. Recordings are deleted from your phone as soon as they have been sent.

Trackers. The names, colours and icons of the trackers you create.

My List. Suggestions you save in the Inspire tab.

Account information. If you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique account identifier and the email address you choose to share (Apple lets you hide your real address). We do not receive your password.

Nothing else. Tracebook does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device advertising identifiers, and does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use it

  • To show you your own moments, trackers and saved list, and to keep them in sync across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To generate suggestions in the Inspire tab. Your recent moments (from the last two weeks) are sent to the AI service to produce a few recommendations. This feature is only available when you are signed in.
  • To fetch artwork for those suggestions. We look up covers and photos using only the title of the recommended book, film, album, podcast or a short generic phrase such as "tennis court" — never your moments or your identity.
  • To respond when you contact us.

Where your information lives

On your phone. As a guest, everything is stored locally on your device and nowhere else. If you later sign in, the moments on the phone are copied to your account once and then removed from local storage.

In our cloud. When you are signed in, your moments, trackers and saved list are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Access is restricted to your own account.

Services we rely on

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and app distribution through the App Store and TestFlight.
  • Supabase — database, authentication and the server functions that process your entries.
  • Google Gemini API — turns your speech or text into moments and produces Inspire suggestions. Requests are sent from our server; Google's use of API data is governed by its Gemini API terms.
  • Open Library, Google Books, Apple's iTunes Search, and Pexels or Unsplash — artwork for Inspire cards, queried with titles only.

Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy policy. We do not share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Your moments, trackers and saved list are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Voice recordings and raw text are not retained after processing. Inspire suggestions are cached on your device for a day.

Your choices

  • Use it without an account. Guest mode keeps everything on your phone.
  • Edit or delete moments at any time from the Today tab. Deleting a tracker deletes every moment in it.
  • Export your data from the Me tab.
  • Sign out from the Account screen. Your cloud data stays in your account until you delete it.
  • Delete your account. Open the Account screen from the Me tab and choose "Delete account". This immediately and permanently removes your account and every moment, tracker and saved item in it. You can also email us at hello@tracebook.app and we will do it for you.

Children

Tracebook is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Cloud data is protected by row-level access rules so only your signed-in account can read or change it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@tracebook.app.

Support

Tracebook for iOS

Something confusing or broken? Email babyrockproject@gmail.com. It helps to mention your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong. You can also tap Help & Feedback on the Me tab, which starts an email with those details filled in.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. As a guest, everything stays on your phone. Signing in with Apple syncs your moments across devices and unlocks the Inspire tab.

I signed in and my earlier moments vanished.

They didn't. Moments logged as a guest are copied into your account the first time you sign in, then removed from the phone's local storage. If you sign out, the phone shows an empty log until you sign in again.

How do I edit or delete a moment?

Tap a moment to edit it. Press and hold any moment to enter edit mode, then tap the red minus to delete. Delete is also at the bottom of the editor.

How do I log a past day?

Swipe left or right on the Today tab, use the arrows beside the date, or tap the calendar. Anything you type or say goes into the day on screen.

How do I export my data?

Me tab → Export data. It hands a JSON file of all your trackers and moments to the iOS share sheet.

How do I delete my account?

Me tab → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it. Or email us and we'll do it.

Why does the microphone need permission?

Only to record what you say; the recording is sent for transcription and not kept. See the Privacy Policy.

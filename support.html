How Tracebook works

You talk (or type) about your day, and Tracebook turns it into short threads you can look back on. Two words cover most of it:

Book
One thing you keep track of — your days, a hobby, the people in your life, your training, your symptoms, or anything you describe. Each book has a name, an icon and a colour, and is built on a template that shapes how its days look.
Thread
One small fact: oatmeal, 20 minutes, nailed the F chord, she seemed happy. Say a sentence with four things in it and you get four threads, sorted onto the day's page. Threads are yours to edit, move or delete.

Day to day

  1. Create a book. Me → Books → New (or the book picker at the top of Trace). Give it a name, pick a template, then an icon and a colour. Your first book is My Life.
  2. Tell it about your day. On the Trace tab, hold the green mic and speak, or type into Add a thread…. Your words become threads on the day you are looking at.
  3. Read the day. Threads are grouped by what they are about. Tap one to edit it, or hold the mic in the editor and say the change. Press and hold a thread to enter edit mode, where you can delete it or drag it into another group.
  4. Move between days. Use the week strip, the arrows beside the date, or the calendar; Today brings you back. Anything you add goes into the day on screen.
  5. Get inspired. The Inspire tab suggests books, films, music, places, food and more, drawn from what you've been up to lately. It needs an account.
  6. Everything else is on Me: your account and export, books, settings, help, about, privacy and terms.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. As a guest, everything stays on your phone. Signing in — with Apple, Google, or a link sent to your email — keeps your books in sync across devices and unlocks the Inspire tab.

I signed in and my earlier threads vanished.

They didn't. Threads logged as a guest are moved into your account the first time you sign in. If you sign out, the phone shows an empty log until you sign in again.

How do I edit, move or delete a thread?

Tap a thread to edit it, or hold the mic in the editor and say the change (“make that 30 minutes”). Press and hold any thread to enter edit mode: tap the red minus to delete, or hold a thread a moment and drag it into another group — holding near the top or bottom scrolls the day.

A whole group has the wrong name — or two groups should be one.

In edit mode, tap the group's title. Rename it, or merge it into another group, for this day only or for every day in the book.

Something landed under Other. Why?

Other is where a thread goes when it isn't clearly about anything in the book. Tap the thread and give it the right place; the book takes the hint next time.

Can I change a book's template or labels?

The template — how a book's days are laid out — is set when the book is created, so its pages stay consistent; you can rename a book and change its icon and colour any time, and create another book with a different template. Labels are looser: the ones you see are a guide, threads get whatever short label fits, and you can change any thread's label by tapping it.

How do I log a past day?

Swipe the week strip, use the arrows beside the date, or tap the calendar. Anything you type or say goes into the day on screen. Days with threads show dots under the date.

Why does the microphone need permission?

Only to hear you while the mic is held. Recordings aren't kept — see the Privacy Policy.

Why does Inspire need an account?

Suggestions are made from your recent threads, which we only do for accounts. They change over time, so the same idea won't keep coming back.

How do I export my data?

Me → your account card → Export data. Choose a spreadsheet (CSV) or a full backup (JSON) of every book and thread; it goes to the iOS share sheet.

Is my data backed up?

When you're signed in, yes — your books and threads live in your account, safely backed up. Guests should use Export data now and then: a guest's data exists only on the phone.

How do I delete my account?

Me → your account card → Advanced → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it. Or email us and we'll do it.

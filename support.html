How Tracebook works

You talk (or type) about your day, and Tracebook turns what you said into short, sorted threads you can look back on. Four words cover most of it:

Book
One thing you trace — your days, your hobbies, the people in your life, your training, your symptoms, or anything you describe. Every book has a name, an icon, a colour and a template that decides how its threads are organised. Book names are unique.
Thread
One fact, the unit of everything: oatmeal, 20 minutes, nailed the F chord, she seemed happy. A sentence with four facts in it becomes four threads. Threads are yours to edit or delete.
Round
One send — a voice clip or a typed line. A round can add threads to several boxes at once.
Box
Where threads live on a day's page. In a Life book the boxes are six fixed categories (Food, Routine, Exercise, Location, Wellbeing, Other). In every other book they are subjects named from what you say — Programming, Jack, Lift, Migraine — and a subject box carries its threads as label: value pairs (duration: 20 minutes, noticed: seemed happy). Life threads carry details instead: time, duration and a note.

Day to day

  1. Create a book. Me → Books → New (or the book picker at the top of Trace). Give it a name, pick a template, then an icon and a colour. Your first book is a Life book called My Life.
  2. Tell it about your day. On the Trace tab, hold the green mic and speak, or type into Add a thread…. Each round is turned into threads and sorted into boxes on the day you are looking at.
  3. Read the day. Boxes are grouped by category or subject; tap a thread to edit it, or say a correction and the thread is refined by voice. Press and hold to enter edit mode and delete.
  4. Move between days. Use the week strip, the arrows beside the date, or the calendar; Today brings you back. Anything you add goes into the day on screen.
  5. Get inspired. The Inspire tab draws on the last two weeks of threads across all your books and suggests books, films, music, places, food and more. It needs an account.
  6. Everything else is on Me: your account and export, books, settings (reminder, default book), help, about, privacy and terms.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. As a guest, everything stays on your phone. Signing in — with Apple, Google, or a link sent to your email — syncs your books and threads across devices and unlocks the Inspire tab.

I signed in and my earlier threads vanished.

They didn't. Threads logged as a guest are copied into your account the first time you sign in, then removed from the phone's local storage. If you sign out, the phone shows an empty log until you sign in again.

How do I edit or delete a thread?

Tap a thread to edit it, or hold the mic in the editor and say the change (“make that 30 minutes”). Press and hold any thread to enter edit mode and tap the red minus to delete; Delete is also at the bottom of the editor.

Why did a thread land in Other?

Other collects what fits no box: a Life thread outside the six categories, or a fact in another book with no subject around it. Tap the thread and change its category or subject, and the book remembers the subject for next time.

Can I change a book's template or labels?

Not yet. A book's template and labels are fixed once it is created, so its threads stay consistent; you can rename a book and change its icon and colour at any time, and create another book with a different template. How this book works (Me → Manage books → ⋯) shows the current design.

What does Custom do with what I say?

Your description (and any answers) is sent to our server and on to the AI service to design the book. The design and your description are kept on the book so you can see them later; nothing else about it is stored. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I log a past day?

Swipe the week strip, use the arrows beside the date, or tap the calendar. Anything you type or say goes into the day on screen. Days with threads are marked with a dot.

Which voice engine am I using?

By default your iPhone transcribes what you say (Apple's speech recognition) and Tracebook sends the text. Some accounts use Gemini for voice instead, where the clip itself is sent to our server to be transcribed and understood in one step. Either way, the audio is not kept.

Why does the microphone need permission?

Only to hear what you say while the mic is held. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to it.

Why does Inspire need an account, and why do suggestions not repeat?

Inspire sends your recent threads to the AI service, which we only do for accounts. It remembers which suggestions you have already seen, so the same one is not shown again for a good while and the mix keeps changing.

How do I export my data?

Me → your account card → Export data. Choose a spreadsheet (CSV) or a full backup (JSON) of every book and thread; it goes to the iOS share sheet.

Is my data backed up?

When you are signed in, your books and threads live in our database, which is copied every night and each copy is kept for 14 days. Guests should use Export data for a backup — a guest's data exists only on the phone.

How do I delete my account?

Me → your account card → Advanced → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it. Or email us and we'll do it.
